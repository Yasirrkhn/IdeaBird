IdeaBird Terms

IdeaBird turns user-provided screenshot text into social content drafts. You are responsible for reviewing, editing, and verifying anything you publish.

Content Moderation Policy

We don't generate amplified versions of unverified political allegations or health misinformation.

IdeaBird may block generation when uploaded text appears to contain unverified allegations presented as fact, conspiracy claims, or content designed to spread misinformation.

Responsible Use

  • Do not use IdeaBird to spread false or misleading claims.
  • Do not present generated drafts as verified reporting without independent verification.
  • Do not use generated content to harass, impersonate, or mislead people.

Generated Drafts

Outputs are drafts, not endorsements or factual verification. Always check names, dates, claims, numbers, and context before publishing.
